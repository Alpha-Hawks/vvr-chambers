## 1. What this document is, and is not

This memo sets out **our proposed compliance position** as the team building your website, so that you can verify it before we proceed.

**It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for your own verification with the Telangana State Bar Council.** We are a design and development team, not your advisers on professional conduct. Where this memo states our understanding of Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules, we ask you to confirm or correct it. Where we are unsure, we say so.

The declaration furnished to the Bar Council will be **yours**, not ours. We have therefore written this memo so that every judgement call reaching that declaration is one you have made on the record.

---

## 2. The posture we propose

We propose to build strictly within the proviso to Rule 36 — publishing only the prescribed particulars, for each advocate individually.

**Our understanding of what may be published, per advocate:**

- Name
- Address, telephone number, email address
- Enrolment number and date of enrolment
- State Bar Council of original enrolment, and on whose roll the name currently stands
- Bar Association membership
- Academic and professional qualifications
- Areas of practice — **listed, not described**

**Please confirm this list is correct and complete.** If your reading differs in either direction, we will build to your reading.

---

## 3. What we are deliberately leaving out

We are excluding the following, notwithstanding that some appear on other counsel's websites:

| Excluded | Reason |
|---|---|
| Client testimonials | Solicitation |
| Case results or outcomes | Solicitation; also confidentiality |
| Rankings, awards, "leading" / "best" | Self-laudatory |
| Client names or logos | Confidentiality and consent |
| Descriptive copy for practice areas | Beyond "areas of practice" |
| Articles, insights, blog | Beyond prescribed particulars |
| Publications, speaking engagements | Beyond prescribed particulars |
| "20+ years' experience" as a claim | Date of enrolment conveys this factually |

We recognise this is more conservative than common practice among Indian senior counsel and firms. That is deliberate, and it was your instruction. **If you would prefer a less restrictive position, tell us — it materially expands what the site can do.**

## 4. Decisions required from you

### 4.1 Photography

A portrait is **not** among the prescribed particulars. It is also near-universal on senior counsel websites, and it is the fastest way for an instructing advocate to confirm they have found the right person.

We have designed the site to work with or without portraits, so this decision does not delay us.

- ☐ Include a portrait of me
- ☐ Include portraits of me and of participating juniors
- ☐ No photography
- ☐ I will confirm after checking with the Bar Council

### 4.2 The separation notice — **requires your approval of exact wording**

As a Senior Advocate designated under Section 16 of the Advocates Act, 1961, you cannot accept a brief or instructions directly from a client. Your juniors, not being designated Seniors, can.

The site must make this unmistakable, or it risks implying that you accept direct instructions. We propose a persistent notice — not a footnote — wherever a reader could confuse the two routes.

**Proposed wording, for your amendment:**

> [ADVOCATE NAME] is a Senior Advocate designated under Section 16 of the Advocates Act, 1961, and does not accept briefs or instructions directly from clients. Instructions must be received through an instructing advocate. For availability and dates, please contact the chambers clerk.

- ☐ Approved as drafted
- ☐ Approved with the amendments marked
- ☐ I will draft this myself

### 4.3 Collective chambers branding — a risk we want on the record

Each advocate publishing their own prescribed particulars is, on our reading, clean. Presenting sixteen advocates **together under a single chambers identity** is the part we are less certain about: the individual entries are compliant, but the collective framing may read as the chambers advertising.

We do not have a confident view on this. **Please raise it specifically with the State Bar Council.**

- ☐ Proceed with a collective chambers identity
- ☐ Present each advocate individually, with no collective branding
- ☐ Deferred pending Bar Council guidance
